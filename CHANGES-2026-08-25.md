## 1. Search

A field at the top of Profile, on every surface and in every account state.
Results show the setting, its description and which surface holds it; taking one
navigates there, opens the section if it is collapsed, and marks the row.
`/` or `⌘K` focuses it; arrows and Enter select; Escape closes.

**The index is derived from the same definition that builds the rail** rather
than maintained beside it. That is what makes PR-195 hold by construction: a
surface cannot be searchable and un-navigable, or navigable and unsearchable,
because there is only one list. A prospect searching `nominee` or `bank account`
gets nothing, because the rail gives them neither.

A short synonym list covers terms customers are handed elsewhere — PAN, BOID,
IFSC, DDPI, 2FA — so the word a customer types resolves even where the surface
does not use it.

**Open:** search emits no events. No `sub_module`, no name for a query, a
zero-result query, or a result taken. A zero-result query is the most direct
evidence available of a customer looking for something Profile does not offer,
and it is currently discarded. Raised as **P-31a**.

## 2. Add funds card removed

It rendered above every Profile surface, which made it the only standing
money-movement control in a section §1.4 defines as *not a growth surface*. DP-6
already forbids promotion in the rail; a funding CTA on the same page as a
customer's masked PAN is that argument one step further in.

**§7.13's in-journey pre-flight is untouched** (PR-103 … PR-105) — that top-up is
raised by a charge the customer has already agreed to, not offered to someone who
came to read their PAN.

Consequences, recorded rather than absorbed:

- PR-161a **withdrawn**, and listed in §7.14 as withdrawn-not-pending — it is the
  only row in that table where the surface was removed on purpose.
- **AT-P-65 and AT-P-66 struck**, not failed. Counting a test against a surface
  removed deliberately is how a deviation list stops being read.
- §7.4 held the last verified account un-removable because *Add funds needs one*.
  That reason no longer exists. The rule stands on **PR-27** instead — the primary
  account is the settlement destination, and an account with no verified bank has
  nowhere for a payout, a closure balance or a mandated settlement to go.
- Analytics: Profile now emits nothing under `funds`. The mapping is retained
  because the in-journey top-up still raises it.

`addFundsCard()` and the addfunds flow remain in source with no surface, matching
how every prior removal in this build was handled.

## 3. Renames

**YOU → Account details.** *YOU* named the reader rather than the contents, which
is the one thing every group in the rail already had in common. The eyebrow above
Personal details, Contact details and Demat details follows the group name, as it
does for every other group.

**Basic details → Personal details.** This closes a four-month deviation rather
than opening one: §7.3 has called that surface *Personal & KYC details* since
v1.0.0 while the build called it *Basic details*. §7.3 keeps the full name as its
heading — a rail entry is a label, not a description.

⚠️ **P-31, and it is the expensive part.** The rail now reads **ACCOUNT DETAILS**
above **ACCOUNT** above **ACCOUNT SERVICES**. Support §10.6b publishes paths of
the form `Profile → Account …` — `HC-ACC-08`, `HC-DMT-08` and GEN-10 all say
`Profile → Account` — and **AT-P-01 requires every published path to resolve at
the name it is spoken at**. That path now has three plausible destinations.
Either the second group is renamed to something that is not a prefix of the
first, or the published answers are made specific. Compounds **P-19**, which
already records that `Profile → Account` names neither freeze nor closure since
PR-134a split them. Owner: Content + Support.

## 4. Closure CTA

*Close* alone is the word a dialog's dismiss button carries. On the one control
that ends the account, being read as *dismiss this* rather than *end my account*
is unrecoverable. Now reads **Close account**. PR-198 states the general rule: a
destructive control names its object.

## 5. Bank verification as a scenario

Prototype affordance only — no requirement changes.

Previously the seed data carried a permanent ICICI account stuck at *Being
verified*. That made it the only one of four outcomes anyone could see, it could
not be cleared, and an addition permanently in progress reads as a stuck account
rather than the transient state it is.

Profile now seeds **one verified account, nothing in progress**. The other states
are reachable from the prototype bar:

| Option | State |
| :--- | :--- |
| One account — nothing being added *(default)* | HDFC only |
| Addition in progress — being verified | ICICI added, *Being verified* + *What is happening*, rail dot on |
| Addition succeeded — both verified | ICICI verified |
| Addition failed — verification failed | ICICI failed, *Why it failed* + Try again |

This makes **PR-29** demonstrable — a pending *or failed* verification carries its
reason and its next step — which previously could only be shown for the pending
half.

⚠ **A failed verification raises no attention item and no rail dot; a pending one
does.** Since the failed state is the one that needs the customer to act, that
ordering is backwards. It is pre-existing logic (`attention()` tests
`status==='pending'` only), now visible because the state is reachable. No open
item raised — flagged for a decision.
